FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Bitcoin DCA, the tool, security, and getting started.

A major advantage of Bitcoin DCA is that you don't need to decide the best time to buy every day. By investing a fixed amount regularly, you automatically buy more BTC when prices are low and less when prices are high — averaging your purchase price over time. It also promotes disciplined, automated investing that removes emotion from the equation.
The tool can set up automated recurring buy orders on your exchange — even if the exchange doesn't natively support this feature. It also lets you automate sell orders for your exit strategy, set limit buy/sell prices, and automate withdrawals to your preferred wallet. All through your exchange API — we never hold your funds.
Anytime. Your funds are always in your exchange account, not ours. You can pause, modify, or stop your DCA strategy at any moment with no penalties or commitments. Automated withdrawals to your wallet can also be triggered by balance thresholds you set.
None. There are no lock-in periods, minimum investment requirements, or cancellation fees. You can stop your DCA strategy whenever you want. Creating an account is free, and you only pay exchange trading fees as a percentage of each transaction — typically well under 1%.
Security is layered. First, we strongly recommend enabling IP restriction on your API key — this ensures the key can only be used from our server. Second, only use exchanges that support withdrawal address whitelisting, so BTC can only go to addresses you've approved. Third, 2FA is required for any withdrawal automation. We can only place orders — we cannot move funds to arbitrary addresses.
No. BTC DCA never receives or holds your money. All funds stay in your chosen exchange account. We connect via API to place orders on your behalf — nothing more. Think of us as a scheduling layer on top of your exchange, not a financial intermediary.
