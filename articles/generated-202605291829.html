The math of buying the absolute top

Let's look at what actually happens if you start buying at the peak. Most people think that if you buy at an all-time high, you are doomed to hold bags for years. But if you are using dollar-cost averaging (DCA), the math works out completely differently.

If you put a lump sum in at the peak, yes, you are down. But if you split that money into weekly buys, you buy fewer fractions of a coin at the top, and significantly more as the price drops. Your average cost basis falls rapidly.
