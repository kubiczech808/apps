My personal rule for market uncertainty

I have one concrete rule that keeps me from checking the price every five minutes: I never change my buy amount based on what I read in the news. Not once. If the economy is tightening and rates are high, my buy stays the same. If we are in a mania and everyone is calling for 100k, my buy stays the same.

Here is a simple checklist I use to stay on track:

  • Does my current buy amount hurt my monthly cash flow? If no, keep going.
  • Is my Bitcoin held on a hardware wallet? If not, move it to a [Trezor](https://bit.ly/buy-trezor-t).
  • Am I tempted to sell because of a macro headline? If yes, close the browser and walk away for 24 hours.

If you struggle with the emotional weight of these decisions, it might be time to stop manual trading.
